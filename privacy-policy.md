# Privacy Policy for AngleIyagi

**Last updated: October 21, 2026**

## Overview
AngleIyagi is an angle meter for Android.

## Data Collection
AngleIyagi does **not** collect, store, or transmit any personal data to the developer. Sensor readings are used on the device only. It has no accounts, no analytics, and no advertising.

## Network Use
AngleIyagi does not need an internet connection and does not send any data to external servers.
